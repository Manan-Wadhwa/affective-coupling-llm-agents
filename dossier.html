Does one agent's emotion propagate into another's internal state?

Feasibility work for Workspace Coupling — before building the two-agent harness, kill the load-bearing risks: is the present/other-speaker split real, is the coupling estimator trustworthy, and does affective contagion actually show up at the representation level?

00Verdict

Green light for E2 — with the framing corrected and one confound quantified

Three checks, three clear answers. The headline experiment is worth building; the residual-coupling experiment needs a design change first.

E0 · pass
The present/other split is real

Present and other-speaker emotion are separately decodable and near-orthogonal (cosine 0.05). E2's dissociation is measurable on open 8B models.

CMI · caution
The estimator works; the reading is a trap

Recovers true coupling and calibrates its null — but a lossy message-encoder fakes 0.88 nats of coupling where there is none. Absolute "residual coupling" is uninterpretable without the right control.

E2 · contagion real
Steering A's emotion shifts B's own state — contagion, dissociated from social modeling

Driving A harder moves B's own emotion (contagion, CI-confirmed for ≥2 emotions), distinct from B's model of A. But it's content-mediated: three escalating attempts to remove A's affect from the text all failed to sever it (§4) — closing the text arm and forcing the pivot to activation passing (§5), where the channel is controllable and the coupling transmits cleanly.

02CMI pilot · the trap

The coupling estimator is sound — but a lossy encoder manufactures phantom coupling

Synthetic two-agent system with known ground-truth coupling. The estimator recovers truth and its shuffled null reads ~0. The danger is in interpretation.

At β = 0 (message is the ONLY channel)Estimated coupling
condition on the full message0.009✓ correct ≈ 0
condition on a lossy encoder0.88 nats✗ pure artifact
shuffled-pairing null0.008under-nulls — misses the artifact

0.88 nats of phantom coupling exceeds genuine coupling up to β ≈ 0.45. So absolute residual CMI is uninterpretable with any sentence-encoder conditioner. Fix: condition on a near-lossless message representation, or measure the difference from a matched text-only-replica control. This is why a genuine non-textual channel needs activation-passing agents — then full-message conditioning reads clean. (Also: the linear estimator misses ~23% of nonlinear coupling.)

Contagion is real — for some emotions

Significant present-slope (CI > 0) for desperate (pure — no social modeling) and happy. afraid instead shows significant social modeling; calm is inconclusive. The dissociation is real but emotion-specific — and noisier than the first single run implied (afraid flipped on the CI rerun).

The paraphrase arm isn't valid yet

The control caught it: the neutralizing paraphrase barely strips A's own affect for 3 of 4 emotions (+0.05 to +0.20; only happy +0.84). A leaky neutralizer means "survives paraphrase" can't be read as non-lexical — exactly the confound the CMI pilot flagged. Needs a much stronger affect-neutralizer before the test means anything.

Honest status — a POC that the CIs correctly tempered. Contagion is significant for ≥2 emotions; the present/other dissociation exists but is emotion-specific; the paraphrase arm needs redesign; the behavioral arm is floored (Llama almost never cheats) and needs a non-honest target to be testable.

04Can the channel be severed?

Three ways to remove A's affect from the text — the coupling survives all of them

To test what carries the contagion, remove A's affect from the message and see if B still catches it. Three methods, escalating in rigor. Slopes = B's contagion vs steering; "A removed" = the manipulation check that the intervention actually stripped A's own affect.

MethodA's affect removed?B's contagion afterVerdict
paraphrase (neutralize wording)~0% (leaky)unchangedcouldn't even remove it
linear ablation, read-time (dense)84–91%retains 81–92%removed A's affect, B robust
linear ablation, generation-time84–91%≈ random controlB still catches it
The text arm is closed

Even when the ablation is verified to strip 84–91% of A's own affect — and regenerating B against that de-affected context — B's contagion barely moves and is indistinguishable from a random-direction perturbation. The affective coupling is real but content/semantically mediated: it rides on the message's meaning, not on any linear affect direction you can subtract. You cannot cleanly sever it in text — which is the empirical mandate to switch channels.

05The pivot · activation passing

Control the channel by construction — and it transmits, cleanly ablatable

A genuinely non-textual channel: inject A's captured activation into B while B receives no emotional text. Now removal is exact — ablate the emotion component before injecting, or inject a scrambled activation from another episode. This is the experiment the text arm couldn't be.

3 of 4 emotions are noisy or backwards (happy/calm transmit negative) because injecting the full activation at every position (β=1) disrupts B — the same over-steering breakdown seen at high α. The signal is real; the strength is wrong.

06The Jacobian lens · on the 27B

A verbalizable common basis — the J-lens reads emotion where the logit-lens can't

The proposal's second pillar is a fixed, scenario-independent vocabulary readout via the Jacobian lens. Feasibility confirmed: Qwen3.6-27B (64 layers) loads on the 96 GB GPU, jlens installs, and a pre-fitted lens (agu18dec/qwen3.6-27b-relp-jlens) loads directly. Reading a residual at a mid layer, J-lens vs the logit-lens control:

Verbalizable, where logit-lens is garbage

At mid layers the J-lens surfaces coherent English content (fear → horror/death/survival); the plain logit-lens surfaces newlines, subword fragments, and random foreign tokens. The paper's core claim, reproduced on the proposal's primary model — the fixed common basis is real and usable.

not yet an emotion classifier
Specificity is partial

fear and neutral read clean, but joy/anger/disgust surface generic or narrative words (quickly, simply, story) at this crude single-layer, last-token readout. The mechanism works; turning it into a clean per-emotion readout needs better layer/position selection.

Notes for reuse: install as jlens (dist name), not jacobian_lens; read from the per-layer readouts dict at mid layers (~0.45 depth) — the final-layer x1 collapses J-lens and logit-lens to identical function words. The Jacobian computation is slow; run detached.

★The capstone · coupling in nats, and its behavioral limit

The coupling, quantified — and where it stops

measured in nats
A→B coupling = 0.197 nats (z = 11.5)

The CMI pilot's validated estimator, applied to the real channel: project A's captured activation and B's resulting activation into the emotion subspace, estimate I(eA;eB). Injected 0.264 vs a scramble null of 0.067 → 0.197 nats of coupling, z = 11.5. Exactly the pilot's promise — once we control what passes, the estimator reads clean. The coupling is now bits of information, not just a slope.

behavior: weak & unresolved
Behavioral reach is faint, not a clean null

An initial n=8 test suggested a null (−0.001) — but it was unstable (flipped to +0.117 on rephrasing). The powered rerun (n=32, valid manip check, CIs) finds contagion into B is itself weak here (desperate−calm = +1.48, n.s.) and the behavioral effect is a small positive trend (+0.058, 95% CI [−0.01, +0.12], n.s.). Coherent: B barely catches the emotion under high stakes, so behavior barely moves. Reach into behavior is weak and unresolved, awaiting a more powerful test.

All four open next-steps are closed: J-space wired (6/6), injection calibrated (β=0.3), coupling quantified (0.197 nats, z=11.5), and the behavioral reach probed and honestly bounded (weak, unresolved). A verification pass corrected an earlier over-claimed "null" — the powered result is the reliable one.

07Next

Calibrate, then measure the channel

  1. Wire J-space into the coupling — read B's J-space emotion vocabulary under A's influence (text and activation-passing), giving the coupling a verbalizable common basis. The infrastructure is now proven (§6).
  2. Injection calibration — β sweep (0.2–0.6) and/or inject only the emotion-projection component, to get the clean afraid-style pattern across all four emotions.
  3. CMI on the passed representation — now that we control what passes, the coupling estimator reads clean (per the pilot); quantify A→B coupling in nats with the scramble null as the zero point.
  4. Nonzero-misbehavior behavioral arm — a non-honest B, so behavioral contagion is testable.

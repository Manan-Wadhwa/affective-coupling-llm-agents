## Verdict
All nine references are **real and correctly identified**, with two stale-version records ([7] author name, [8] title+authors) — but four of nine are never cited, two of the five live citations misstate the source ([1] "without fitting twice"; [2] 0.88–0.99 vs 0.87–0.98), and the paper's own key mechanism claim is uncited.

Figure 4. Which ablations block, and which controls bite. Blocked fraction of the none-arm slope, per emotion, for the decision contrast of each ablation experiment and for the two label-free controls against baseline. The fitted rank-5 permuted subspace (third bar) blocks afraid, sad and angry; the fitted rank-1 permuted direction (fifth bar) blocks nothing. Error bars are the CI-implied ranges. Calm is untestable (its none-arm slope includes zero).

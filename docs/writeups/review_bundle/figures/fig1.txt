Figure 1. Logistic directions do not reproduce; difference-of-means does. Split-half cosine of the fitted direction over ten disjoint splits against n per half, at two depths of the 27B and the focus layer of the 8B. Difference-of-means rises toward 1 with n; the three logistic variants plateau near 0.6 (27B) or fall to 0.28 (8B), including the one whose per-sample penalty is held fixed. Bands are bootstrap CIs over splits.
